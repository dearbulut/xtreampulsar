# 💜 Support XtreamPulsar

XtreamPulsar is built and maintained by **[Bulutworks](https://github.com/dearbulut)** and is
**source-available and free to self-host**. There is no paywall, no license fee for personal or
in-house use, and no telemetry.

If the project saves you time or money, a donation helps cover the things that are not free:
test servers, domains, CDN/bandwidth for the demo, and the hours spent on issues and releases.

**Free ways to help, which matter just as much:**

- ⭐ **Star the repo** — it is the single biggest driver of new users.
- 🐛 **Report bugs** with logs, or open a feature request.
- 📖 **Improve the docs** — even a one-line README fix is welcome.
- 📣 **Tell other operators** about it.
